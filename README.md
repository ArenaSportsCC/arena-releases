# arena-releases
Windows installers and updates for the Arena
Arena Sports Command Center gives you one place to follow your favorite teams on game day. Each team gets its own Command Center with a featured matchup and league scores. When several games are on, open the fullscreen Mega-Board for scores across your favorite leagues or pick up to three matchups to watch side by side on the Three-Game Jumbotron.

Arena covers the NFL, college football, NHL, NBA, MLS, and select international soccer leagues and cups. You can choose which leagues refresh automatically and see scoring alerts and in-game stats where coverage is available.

Arena is available on the web and as a Windows app. Access is invite-only, and your favorite teams and league update preferences sync across both when you sign in with the same Arena account.
